# The 90-day Term 4 plan

## The number

I will book 60 qualified meetings in the 90 days to mid-December, against a run rate of 52 in the same window at today's send volume and conversion. The arithmetic:

- **Schools touched.** 3,682 school touches across 225 AU sequences, plus 244 schools emailed by hand, is 3,926. The email cache behind them starts in July 2025, about 14 months, so near 280 a month. Ninety days is 840. I plan on 800.
- **Meetings per school touched.** Those 3,926 touches produced 634 meetings, 16.1 percent. 800 at that rate is 129 booked.
- **Taking out the double counting.** A school worked by three sequences counts three times, in the touches and in the meetings. A third off: 86 booked.
- **Qualification.** 60 percent of AU leads reaching a booked meeting qualify. That is 52, the run rate.
- **What move one adds.** The weakest large sequence turned 134 school touches into 3 meetings. At 16.1 percent it would have produced 22: 19 more, 13 after the same third comes off, 8 once qualified. That is an assumption about redirected volume, not a measurement.

52 plus 8 is 60. The range is 52 if nothing I change works, and 77 if the double counting is smaller than I assume. At 22 percent of booked meetings becoming wins, 60 qualified is roughly 100 booked, about 22 wins, about $163,000 at the 2026 average deal of $7,394. Winning deals close in a median 27 days, so most of what is booked by mid-November closes inside the window.

In 2025, 204 of 284 wins landed on or after 4 September, 70 percent of the year, worth $1,654,495 of the $2,348,300 full year. 2026 to 3 September has 85 wins worth $628,515, against 80 worth $693,805 in the same window of 2025: the same number of deals for less money. ## The three moves

**One. Move the volume off the weakest sequence.** Pause sequence 299760938 and put its list and its send budget onto the hand-written shape the team already runs. It made 1,030 sends to 134 schools for 3 meetings and 1 win. The spread across the 225 sequences is enormous and none of it is proof: different lists, months and targeting, and a slow sequence reads far worse at 14 days than at 60. This is the largest block of send volume with the least to show, so it is where I start. Worth at current conversion: 13 more booked meetings, 8 qualified, about 3 wins, roughly $22,000. Me with the Sales Manager, first fortnight.

**Two. A three-day clock on silence, but only where silence is a warning.** Any deal from cold outreach or an ABM list with no reply in three days enters a follow-up queue and is flagged to a manager at day ten. Deals arriving through Free Preview or Contact Sales, a principal conference, or a Catholic feeder-school or diocese connection are exempt. Across 842 closed deals, a reply inside three days goes with a 46.9 percent win rate on 559 deals, fifteen days or more with 16.3 percent on 49. Silence itself is not bad: of the 167 that never replied early, the 83 on warm paths won 51.8 percent and the 84 from ordinary outbound 36.9 percent, under the 43.4 percent average. That split is read off campaign names, so a few deals sit on the wrong side of it. None of this is in the number above, because it adds no meetings. If the silent outbound group reached 43.4 percent, that is about 5 more wins per 84 such deals, roughly $40,000, and that is an assumption. Me to build it, the SDRs to work it, live from week two.

**Three. Stamp every send with the angle it used.** Three fields at enrolment: the angle, who it is aimed at, the version. Then enough volume behind the social media ban angle to settle it. Across 842 closed deals no angle separates winners from losers once we compare like with like by grouping deals by how much conversation was logged, and the closest thing to a positive signal is the social media ban, which came up in 19 deals and needed 20 to count. Worth this term: nothing. It is the only way the next 90 days end in an answer rather than another backward look. Me with the portal owner, before the first send of October.

## What this plan does not claim

No angle has been proven to win: across 842 closed deals none clears the bar, and the one that came closest is a lead to check, not a finding.

There is no attribution. No message has ever been stamped with which angle it used, so nothing here tells you what to say, only what happened.

All of it is backward-looking. Running anything live before this decision was not permitted, so every figure is read off deals that already closed. These are associations, not proof.

And the inventory only sees schools that became leads, so every rate in it flatters us. That is why I took a third off and signed up to the bottom of the range.
