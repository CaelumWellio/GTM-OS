Alternatives considered: leave cycle zero as the reference population and treat the coverage gap as an open caveat rather than rerunning; extend the cache further before rerunning; grant only one of the two scopes and rerun on a partially backfilled cache.
Why: the 2026-09-16 reversal condition named the missing emails scope and the missing cache coverage as the reason cycle zero could not be trusted as the last word. Both are now fixed, so rerunning immediately was the direct test that condition called for. The result confirms the bias was real, not a rounding artefact, and it does not change what to build on next. The reversal condition recorded on 2026-09-17 in `2026-09-17-first-machine-reversal-condition-met.md` still stands: no hook clears the bar on the full cache either, so the outbound engine inventory remains the next machine.
