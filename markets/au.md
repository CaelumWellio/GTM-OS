# AU

## Footprint

Penetration by segment: Catholic combined 30% (39 of 132), Catholic secondary 21% (66 of 316), Independent combined 15% (117 of 787), government secondary 15% (181 of 1,170), Independent secondary 14% (19 of 138), government combined 10% (52 of 535), Special 5% (28 of 528), primary 0.4% (24 of 6,156). By state: NSW 5.7%, SA 9.1%, QLD 5.4%, WA 5.2%, VIC 4.5%.

Within the secondary and combined universe (2,969 schools), 720 are SAM (serviceable addressable market: 100+ students, not already a customer or in active pipeline, no recent no). Of those, 208 are clean bulk-enrollable and 683 are the H2 2025 swing group (461 refused-meeting, 220 closed-lost, recoverable under a defined re-approach policy).

Source: ~/Documents/GTM Project/docs/gtm-strategy-2026-09/01-hubspot-findings.md; ~/Documents/GTM Project/docs/revops/2026-08-20-sam-tof-synthesis.md.

## Buyer and signer

Per-student annual licence. Deputy principal or head of wellbeing typically champions the purchase, principal signs. Diocese is the only true multi-school buyer. Free Preview (the highest-ACV inbound source) has a broken sign-up: a 14-character password with a number and symbol is a conversion tax on a teacher signing up on a phone at recess.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Average deal and what drives it

Free Preview and Contact Sales average $10.9k in 2026 (18 deals, $196k), the highest-ACV source and the smallest by volume. Full 2026 YTD average across all deals is $7,394 (85 wins, $628,515); full 2025 average was $8,211 (284 wins). Primary wins at 31% versus 46% for secondary, at half the median deal size ($2,498 versus $5,625), even though primary lists connect and book at a higher rate.

Source: ~/Documents/GTM Project/docs/gtm-strategy-2026-09/01-hubspot-findings.md; brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Compliance or discretionary

Discretionary. The buy is on need, not a curriculum mandate: a named wellbeing owner, no structured program of their own, and the system making wellbeing reportable for the first time.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13, Q4.

## Statutory hook

None identified. AU purchases are discretionary rather than tied to a legal or curriculum requirement (see Compliance or discretionary above).

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Public need data

SA Wellbeing and Engagement Collection, NSW Tell Them From Me, Victorian Attitudes to School Survey (via annual reports), My School (ICSEA, funding, staffing, enrolments). Wellio's own 210,000-student benchmark layers on top. Dioceses buy as a unit.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q4.

## Entry route

Two engines: association and conference-anchored campaigns (physical-pack sequences reply at 28-42% versus 2-5% for generic value-prop sequences) and inbound (Free Preview, Contact Sales; highest ACV, lowest volume). On the SAM-gated secondary and combined universe, 208 schools are clean bulk-enrollable now and the 683-school H2 2025 swing group is re-approachable under policy.

Source: ~/Documents/GTM Project/docs/gtm-strategy-2026-09/01-hubspot-findings.md; ~/Documents/GTM Project/docs/revops/2026-08-20-sam-tof-synthesis.md.

## What we do not know

Whether the 683-school H2 2025 swing group converts once re-approached; two-thirds of the wider SAM (479 schools) are "No Response", which the source calls a messaging and channel problem rather than a market problem, and that has not been tested. Evidence linking Wellio's check-in scores to attendance or academic outcomes has not yet been assembled.
