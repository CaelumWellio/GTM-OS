# UK

## Footprint

300-plus schools. The established second market, alongside AU.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q8.

## Buyer and signer

Not established from the named sources. The UK purchase is framed as need-driven compliance rather than a specific champion-led buying role (see Compliance or discretionary below).

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Average deal and what drives it

About $2,000 average, much smaller than AU, driven by PSHE being a curriculum requirement rather than a discretionary spend.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Compliance or discretionary

Compliance-driven. Wellbeing and PSHE is a curriculum requirement, so schools buy "almost on a need not a want basis."

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.

## Statutory hook

PSHE curriculum requirement is the statutory hook: it is what makes the purchase a need rather than a want.

Source: brainstorm 2026-09-15 Q13.
