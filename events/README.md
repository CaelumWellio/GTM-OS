# Events

What this is: the 2026 conference workbook turned into something you can decide from, covering 151 events, what 12 of them returned, and a rubric for the next booking.
Read first: `2026-plan.md`, one page on what I would change about the 2026 plan and why. Then `returns.md` for what the measured events produced, `scoring.md` for the method behind the recommendation, and `what-to-record.md` for the five fields that make next year's answer better than this one.
What it can see: the Australian pipeline, so 12 events with a matched campaign, 8 of them with both a cost and a return.
What it cannot see: 47 UK and 51 international events, whether a delegate list or a speaking slot changes anything, and whether an event caused a deal or merely had its name on the campaign.
The two files in `data/` are meant to be edited by hand: add a row to `campaign-aliases.csv` when a campaign name does not match an event, and fill in `research.csv` as organisers answer.

Source: `events/2026-plan.md`; `events/scoring.md`; `events/returns.md`; `events/efficiency.md`; `events/catalogue.md`; `events/what-to-record.md`.
