## What this can and cannot see

Every figure here divides a measured outcome by the workbook's expected delegate count, so a large conference and a small one sit on the same footing. Expected delegates is a plan from the workbook, not a verified headcount, and is often a round number. A zero cost is a renegotiated waiver, not a free channel, so it carries no cost-per-delegate figure; an event with no cost recorded at all carries none either - both show blank, never zero.
